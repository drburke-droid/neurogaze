Part of the Visual Systems Lab

This simulator is one of 13 programs in the Visual Systems Lab, Dr Robert Burke's collection of interactive vision science tools at Calgary Vision Centre. Each program was built from first principles to explore a question that lacked a satisfying explanation. Some are for patients; others are intentionally technical.

In the lab, they all live on the desktop of a 1990s computer. Open the lab to explore the rest:
